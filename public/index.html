      Glossary

      METARStands for Meteorological Aerodrome Report. The app uses METAR in airport weather evidence and airport risk signals to read live visibility, clouds, wind, gusts, and VFR/MVFR/IFR status.

      NWSNational Weather Service official U.S. weather alerts and forecasts.

      Precipitation probabilityChance that measurable rain or snow occurs during the forecast period.

      GustWind is the steady speed. Gust is a short stronger burst. Gusts matter because sudden crosswinds can affect takeoff, landing, and high-profile vehicles.
